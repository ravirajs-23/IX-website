Milestones

Our Journey

From a private limited startup to a 150+ strong AI-native engineering partner spanning India, North America, Oceania, and the Middle East.

2025 — AI Hackathon & AI Bootcamp

Hosted our first org-wide AI Hackathon and Bootcamp to strengthen practical AI skills across teams, and set up the AI Lab for experimentation and prototyping.

2024 — First Agentic AI Solution

Built and delivered our first agentic AI solution, expanded our fintech footprint with a leading US loan-origination platform, and welcomed our 150th IX'ian.

2023 — Global Expansion

Began participating in global events starting with the Dubai AI and Web3 Festival, and rolled out our first AI assistant for natural language knowledge queries.

2022 — Centre of Excellence

Established a dedicated Centre of Excellence for UX Engineering and strengthened certifications across AWS, Azure, UX, Dynamics 365, and Power Apps.

2021 — Crossing 100

Secured our first clients in Oceania and the Middle East, and crossed the 100-person mark as "Centurions".

2020 — Incorporated in the USA

Successfully registered IncubXperts Inc. as a corporate entity in Delaware, USA, and reached 50 employees.

2020 — Resilience Through COVID

Kept operations running smoothly through the pandemic, grew through the acquisition of a major legal tech client, and expanded services to include Dynamics 365 and Power Apps.

Earlier Years — The Journey Begins

IncubXperts is established as a private limited organization, acquires its first North American client, becomes a NASSCOM member, and delivers its first AI-powered project.
