Our Story So Far

2025
  • IncubXperts embraces its identity as an AI-native engineering partner, integrating AI into how we operate, design, build, and deliver systems with a focus on purpose-built solutions that create real impact.
  • Hosted our first org-wide AI Hackathon and AI Bootcamp to strengthen practical AI skills across teams.
  • Set up the AI Lab as a dedicated space for experimentation, prototyping, and showcasing AI-led solutions.
  • Built and delivered our first agentic AI solution, setting the foundation for a growing pipeline of applied AI work.
  • Delivered applied AI solutions for a range of practical use cases across domains.
  • Began onshore employee visits to support key accounts and deepen collaboration.
  • Expanded our fintech footprint by kicking off a major engagement with a leading US loan-origination platform provider.
  • Welcomed our 150th IX’ian.
2024
  • Kicked off round-the-clock support for a major enterprise.
  • UI toolkit accelerator successfully rolled out.
  • IncubXperts starts participating in global events, beginning with the Dubai AI and Web3 festival.
  • Successfully rolled out our first AI assistant capable of synthesizing knowledge and answering natural language queries.
2023
  • Established a homegrown Performance Review and Appraisal System for unbiased, data-driven, and transparent employee assessment.
  • Established a dedicated Centre of Excellence for UX Engineering.
  • IncubXperts affirms its status as an Equal Opportunity Employer.
  • Strengthened our expertise with certifications in AWS, Azure, UX, Dynamics 365, and Power Apps.
2022
  • Breaking new ground — secured our first clients in Oceania and the Middle East.
  • Centurions — IncubXperts crosses the 100-person mark.
  • IncubXperts Incorporated! — successfully registered as a corporate entity in Delaware, USA.
2021
  • We are 50 strong! Employee milestone achieved with sustainable hiring strategies.
  • All-round growth — we kick off IX Takatak, our 360-degree health initiative focusing on physical, mental, financial and organizational aspects of health.
  • We expanded our line of service offerings to include platforms like Dynamics 365 and Power Apps.
2020

2019

2018
