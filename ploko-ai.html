About Ploko

Ploko is a Netherlands-based digital agency specializing in AI implementation, business automation, web development, SEO, and performance marketing.

Founded by Giovanni Pira and Erik Plomp, Ploko helps businesses across Europe accelerate growth by combining emerging technologies with practical digital strategy.

As the European implementation partner of IncubXperts, Ploko helps bridge the gap between advanced engineering capabilities and successful business adoption.

IncubXperts provides AI-native software engineering expertise, while Ploko supports organizations across the Netherlands and Europe in integrating, implementing, and maximizing the value of these technologies.

A Partnership Built on Complementary Expertise

IncubXperts brings expertise in AI Consulting and Transformation, Agentic AI, AI-augmented product engineering and workflow automation. Ploko contributes deep experience in implementation, user adoption, search visibility, digital marketing, and business growth.

Together, both organizations support the journey from strategy and implementation to measurable business outcomes.
Clients benefit from technical depth and practical execution, helping ensure that technology investments yield impressive ROI.
