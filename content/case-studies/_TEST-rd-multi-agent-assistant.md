A global consumer electronics manufacturer's R&D division was drowning in unstructured research: thousands of papers, patents, and internal lab reports piling up faster than any team could read them. Promising leads sat buried for weeks, and researchers regularly duplicated work that had already been done elsewhere in the org.

IncubXperts designed a multi-agent assistant where specialized agents divide the work: one agent continuously ingests and summarizes new literature & patents, another cross-references findings against the company's own internal R&D archive to flag duplication, and a third ranks and routes surfaced insights to the right team each morning.

The result was a research pipeline that runs continuously in the background, with humans reviewing curated, ranked findings instead of searching for them — turning a months-long discovery bottleneck into a weeks-long one.
