Redesigning a Payment Platform for ADA Accessibility Compliance

Fintech ComplianceDeque’s Axe Accessibility SuiteADA ComplianceUX EngineeringWCAG StandardsDigital Payments PlatformAccessibilityARIA RolesUser ExperienceEnterpriseADA Compliance for FintechInclusive Digital PaymentsQA & Automation

Business Problem

A US-based fintech company faced significant accessibility challenges with its React.js payment platform, which did not meet ADA compliance or WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. The platform's poor keyboard navigation, lack of screen reader support, and inadequate color contrast ratios excluded users with disabilities and posed legal risks. To improve usability and regulatory compliance, the client required a comprehensive accessibility solution.

Technology Solution

IncubXperts implemented a holistic strategy to remediate accessibility gaps and align the platform with modern standards for inclusivity:

  • Accessibility Audit: Conducted an in-depth audit using Deque’s Axe Accessibility Suite to identify WCAG 2.1 AA violations.
  • Semantic and Visual Enhancements: Optimized HTML elements for accessibility, improved color contrast to meet a 4.5:1 ratio, and integrated scalable fonts for better readability.
  • Screen Reader Optimization: Added ARIA roles and live regions to support dynamic content and ensure clear, descriptive labeling.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Enabled logical focus order, keyboard-focusable elements, and skip-to-content links for seamless navigation.
  • Automated Testing: Integrated Axe tools into the CI/CD pipeline for proactive accessibility checks during development cycles.

Technology Stack

  • Framework: React.js
  • Audit Tools: Deque’s Axe Accessibility Suite
  • Compliance Standards: WCAG 2.1 AA

Benefits & Impacts

Enhanced Inclusivity

Delivered an accessible user experience for individuals with disabilities, boosting satisfaction and engagement.

Regulatory Compliance

Achieved ADA and WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, reducing legal and reputational risks.

Increased Engagement

Improved customer satisfaction metrics by 20%, expanding the platform's user base.

Sustained Accessibility

Automated compliance checks within the CI/CD pipeline reduced ongoing development efforts while maintaining accessibility.
