Transforming Community Wellness with a Mobile Solution for an Upscale American Neighborhood

Community WellnessSocial NetworkingFitness Tracking PlatformCross-platform Mobile AppReact NativeGamificationAWSFirebaseAutomated RemindersSmart Community Engagement

Business Problem

Residents of an upscale community in the USA lacked a unified platform to track and manage fitness activities, resulting in reduced participation in community wellness programs. The absence of an integrated solution made it challenging for city administrators to manage logistics and communicate effectively. The city aimed to increase resident engagement and streamline administrative processes through a comprehensive mobile application.

Technology Solution

IncubXperts developed a specialized mobile app for both Android and iOS platforms, complemented by an Admin Tool to enhance fitness tracking and program management.

  • The app allows residents to track their health activities (such as blood pressure, weight, and daily food intake), set personal goals, and earn wellness points for active participation.
  • The Admin Tool enables efficient user management, message handling, and reporting capabilities, streamlining administrative processes and ensuring smooth program operations.
  • Role-based access control, secure authentication, and AWS scalability ensure data privacy, secure administration, and platform growth.
  • Firebase push notifications and cron jobs automate wellness point calculations and user reminders.

Technology Stack

  • React Native
  • Spring Boot
  • Nest JS
  • Angular
  • Docker
  • SQL
  • AWS

Benefits & Impacts

Data-Driven Decisions

The app's analytics provided key insights, helping administrators optimize wellness programs based on resident activity and preferences.

Enhanced Communication

Integrated push notifications led to a 25% increase in timely engagement, ensuring residents stayed informed and motivated.

Improved Administrative Efficiency

The Admin Tool reduced manual administrative tasks by 40%, streamlining user management and reporting, allowing city officials to focus on strategic decisions.

Increased Resident Engagement

The app boosted community wellness program participation by 30%, with residents actively tracking fitness goals and earning wellness points as incentives.

Scalability & Security

With AWS and Firebase, the platform can scale to accommodate growing user bases while ensuring data privacy and security.
