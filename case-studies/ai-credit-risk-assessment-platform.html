Fintech

7 Million Cases, 3000 Users — An AI-Driven Phenomenon in Credit Risk Assessment

An AI-driven web and mobile platform that modernized credit risk assessment workflows, replacing outdated legacy systems with automation, predictive analytics, and fraud detection.

Fintech AI Credit Risk Automation Financial Compliance Predictive Analytics AI Adoption & Strategy Integrations

Business Problem

A financial services company, managing over half of the national credit risk assessment market, struggled with inefficiencies and revenue leakage due to outdated legacy systems. To remain competitive, they required a modern, AI-powered platform to streamline credit risk workflows, reduce operational bottlenecks, and improve financial performance.

Technology Solution

IncubXperts developed a robust, AI-driven web and mobile platform using a Disconnected Architecture to modernize the credit risk assessment process. The solution automates workflows, enhances user engagement, and improves operational efficiency with key features:

  • Cross-Platform Development: Used MAUI to build a mobile app with reusable code for iOS and Android, reducing development efforts.
  • AI-Powered Survey Automation: Automated the conversion of field survey findings into structured audit reports using Generative AI, reducing back-office processing time and enhancing accuracy.
  • Predictive Analytics for Loan Approval: Leveraged AI to calculate loan approval probabilities based on survey and user data, enabling faster and data-driven decisions.
  • Offline Sync and Geo Tracking: Provided uninterrupted app usage and integrated geocoordinate mapping for data authentication and workforce tracking.
  • Dynamic and Intuitive UI: Implemented dynamic form rendering for a flexible, user-friendly interface tailored to business needs.
  • AI-Driven Business Rule Engine: Automated contract verification, ensured adherence to turnaround times, and detected anomalies or potential fraud risks.
  • Real-Time Reporting: Delivered actionable insights with near real-time reporting in multiple formats (PDF, Excel, dashboards) to optimize decision-making and operations.

Technology Stack

Core Technologies: ASP.NET MVC, MS SQL Server, Web API, MAUI, Azure Functions, Google Maps

AI and Data Services: Azure Cognitive Services, OpenAI GPT Models, Azure Machine Learning

Data Management: Azure SQL, Azure Blob Storage, Azure Cosmos DB

Benefits & Impacts

60% Faster Back-Office Processing

Generative AI reduced the time to process survey findings into reports, enhancing efficiency and reducing errors.

Faster Loan Approvals

Predictive analytics improved decision-making and shortened loan processing times.

50% Improved TAT

AI-enhanced automation optimized key workflows and elevated customer service.

Reduced Fraud Risk

Advanced de-duplication and fraud detection mechanisms ensured compliance and operational trust.

Scalable and Adaptable Platform

The solution evolves with user interactions, supporting a growing user base without compromising performance.
