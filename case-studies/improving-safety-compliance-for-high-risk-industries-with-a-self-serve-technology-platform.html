Improving Safety Compliance for High-Risk Industries with a Self-Serve Technology Platform

Public SafetyComplianceAutomated Safety ReportingEmployee Health and SafetyIncident ReportingAzure FunctionsThird-Party IntegrationsTwilioBraintree PaymentsPayment IntegrationSafety & Compliance Management PlatformMobile & Web App

Business Problem

High-risk industries like mining face challenges in ensuring employee safety and meeting regulatory compliance due to manual, error-prone processes for incident reporting and inspections. Delays in generating compliant reports and managing complex workflows increase inefficiencies and risks. The client required an automated, scalable solution to centralize safety management, improve operational efficiency, and ensure compliance.

Technology Solution

IncubXperts developed a centralized safety management platform with automation and offline capabilities to streamline compliance and operations. Key Features:

  • Unified Architecture: Monolithic design consolidated tenant data for scalability and efficiency.
  • Automated Workflows: Included programmatic tenant onboarding, configurable modules, and pro-rated invoicing to streamline operations.
  • Offline Functionality: Mobile app enabled incident logging and reporting without network connectivity.
  • Real-Time Notifications: Azure Functions and Service Bus ensured timely alerts via email, SMS, and push notifications.
  • Robust Security: Multi-level authentication and data encryption ensured secure operations and access control.

Technology Stack

  • Core Technologies: ASP.NET MVC, Xamarin Forms, SQL Server
  • Cloud Services: Azure Functions, Azure Web Jobs
  • Third-Party Integrations: SendGrid, Twilio, Firebase, Braintree

Benefits & Impacts

Seamless Offline Operations

Enabled continuous logging and reporting from remote sites.

Regulatory Compliance

Real-time workflows ensured adherence to government standards.

50% Time Savings

Automated invoicing reduced financial management time and errors.

Enhanced Security

Flexible roles and authentication improved access control and data security.

Scalable Growth

Centralized architecture streamlined onboarding, supporting future expansion.
