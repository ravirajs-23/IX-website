An Employee-Centric Mobile Application for Tracking Meal Expenses for Better Tax Savings

OCR IntegrationTax ComplianceNode.js APIFirebaseMeal Expense ManagementMeal Reimbursement ProgramTax SavingExpense TrackingFlutterProductivityOCR for Finance AutomationPaper Trail DigitizationDigital TransformationCross-platform Mobile App

Business Problem

In Austria, managing tax-saving meal expenses traditionally required manual submission of lunch receipts and data entry, resulting in lost receipts, readability issues, and inefficiencies. This process was time-consuming for employees and HR teams alike. To overcome these challenges, a streamlined solution was needed to simplify and automate meal expense management for improved accuracy and efficiency.

Technology Solution

To address these issues, we developed a comprehensive application with the following key features:

  • A Node.js API for a seamless login system, allowing employees to manage monthly meal budgets easily.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology to extract invoice details like hotel names, bill items, prices, and taxes from uploaded receipts, improving data accuracy and reducing errors.
  • Firebase for secure data storage and organization, ensuring accessibility for employees and HR teams.
  • An Admin Portal for HR, simplifying onboarding, budget allocation, and customized meal plan creation.
  • Integration with company-issued Lunch Cards, enabling automatic transaction recording, real-time tracking, and enhanced financial transparency.

Technology Stack

  • Flutter
  • Node.js
  • Firebase
  • Magento

Benefits & Impacts

Boosted HR Productivity

Automation allowed HR teams to focus more time on strategic initiatives.

Enhanced Efficiency and Cost Savings

Streamlined processes reduced HR overheads significantly.

Improved Tax Compliance

Real-time tracking reduced discrepancies during audits by 25%.

Increased Employee Satisfaction

A user-friendly application boosted engagement and efficiency.
