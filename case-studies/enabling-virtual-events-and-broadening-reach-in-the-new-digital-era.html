Enabling Virtual events and Broadening Reach in the New Digital Era

Social NetworkingVirtual Event PlatformWeb AppReal-Time ChatEmbedded Event AnalyticsFirebase Realtime DatabaseDigital Community Engagement

Business Problem

The COVID-19 pandemic disrupted in-person engagements, compelling a mobile-centric social networking platform for event organizers and participants to adapt. The platform needed to evolve into a web-based application to support virtual events, maintain relevance, and broaden its market reach.

Technology Solution

IncubXperts developed a web-based social networking application tailored for virtual event management. Key highlights include:

  • Live Video Events: Enabled seamless execution of virtual events, including Moderated sessions, Virtual Lounges, and Speed Networking.
  • Real-Time Interaction: Integrated Firebase Realtime Database for live chat and user interaction.
  • Event Insights: Delivered real-time updates on upcoming, ongoing, and past events.
  • Enhanced Connectivity: Added friend-linking features and recommendation algorithms for better social engagement.
  • Advanced Analytics: Embedded detailed networking analytics to measure event engagement and performance.

Technology Stack

Angular, Firebase, HTML

Benefits & Impacts

Expanded Business Model

Transitioned from B2B to B2B2C, broadening market reach and opportunities.

Seamless Adaptation

Successfully shifted from physical to virtual event hosting, ensuring business continuity during the pandemic.

Enhanced Community Engagement

Launched a web platform that fostered inclusivity and deeper community connections.
