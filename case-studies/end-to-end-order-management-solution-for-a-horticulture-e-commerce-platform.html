End-to-end Order Management Solution for a Horticulture E-Commerce Platform

Order Management PortalEcommerceReact.jsAWS LambdaNode.jsScalable Cloud InfrastructureInventory ForecastingAWS IntegrationLegacy Platform EnhancementAmazon SESAWSOrder ProcessingHorticultureInvoice ManagementInvoice ComplianceCloud Transfo

Business Problem

A Canadian horticulture company specializing in high-quality flower bulbs faced operational inefficiencies due to an outdated web system. Challenges included errors in order processing, inventory management, invoicing, and labeling, which hindered their ability to scale operations effectively.

Technology Solution

The company partnered with us to develop a modernized order management portal tailored to their unique needs. The solution included:

  • An intuitive order management system with features like detailed reporting and compliance with Canadian tax laws for accurate invoicing.
  • A robust AWS serverless architecture ensuring scalability and optimized cloud costs.
  • AWS Cognito integration for secure user authentication and access controls.
  • A PostgreSQL database for efficient customer and inventory management, with scalability for future growth.
  • Automated communications powered by Amazon SES, improving efficiency through invoice and order confirmation dispatch.

Technology Stack

React.js, AWS Lambda based API Gateway in Nodejs, Cloud- AWS, CI/CD- AWS Code Build, Amazon Cognito, SES, Ant Design, PostgreSQL

Benefits & Impacts

Data-Driven Decision-Making

Improved data organization and detailed reporting enhance inventory management and support strategic business decisions.

Improved Customer Experience

Automated, timely communications for invoices and order acknowledgments enhance customer satisfaction.

Operational Efficiency Boost

Streamlined order management reduced revenue leakages by 30% and cut shipping costs by up to 45% through optimized split order shipments.

Scalable and Reliable System

A robust, scalable platform minimized processing errors, enabling seamless business growth and enhanced efficiency.

Tax Compliance

Custom-built features ensured compliance with Canadian tax laws, guaranteeing accurate invoicing.
